Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what Auto Stream ("the app"), an internal publishing tool of Augustus Media Holdings Ltd ("Augustus Media", "we", "us"), does with the data it receives from TikTok and the other platforms it connects to. Augustus Media's general Privacy Policy also applies.

Whose data

The app connects only to accounts that Augustus Media owns or manages. It does not collect data about other TikTok users or members of the public.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic): the connected account's ID, display name and profile picture, to show which account is connected.
  • The account's own posts (video.list): each post's ID, caption, date, link and length, to check that a video is not already on the account before it is sent.
  • Posting (video.upload, video.publish): used only to send videos that a team member approved to the connected account, and to read back from TikTok whether each one was posted. Before a direct post, the app also reads the account's posting options, such as who can see its posts and the longest video allowed, to show them on the post screen.
  • Sign-in tokens that TikTok issues when the account owner approves the app.

How we use it

Only to publish the company's approved videos to the company's own accounts, to avoid posting the same video twice, and to keep a record of what was posted for our internal reports. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. We do not share it outside Augustus Media, except with service providers that help us run the app under confidentiality, or where the law requires it.

Where it is kept, and for how long

On the Augustus Media computer that runs the app. Sign-in tokens stay on that computer and are never shown or written to logs. Tokens are kept while the account remains connected and are deleted when access is removed. Records of what was posted are kept for our internal reports.

Removing access

The account owner can remove the app's access at any time in the TikTok app's settings, where the apps connected to the account are listed, or by writing to us at the address below. Once access is removed, the app can no longer read from or post to the account.

Children

The app is used only by Augustus Media staff and does not collect data from children.
