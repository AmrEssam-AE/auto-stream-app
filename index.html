Auto Stream

Auto Stream is an internal publishing tool of Augustus Media Holdings Ltd. Our team uses it to publish the company's own videos, for brands such as Lovin Cairo, to the company's own social media accounts.

What it does on TikTok

  • Connects to TikTok accounts that Augustus Media owns, through TikTok's official sign-in (Login Kit). It never asks for or keeps an account's password.
  • Reads the connected account's own list of posts, so a video that is already on the account is never posted twice.
  • Sends a video to the account only after a team member has approved it, using TikTok's Content Posting API: either as a draft that the team finishes in the TikTok app, or as a post with the settings the team member chose on the post screen.

Who can use it

Only Augustus Media staff. Auto Stream has no public sign-up, and it posts only to accounts that Augustus Media owns.
